Questions worth asking before you choose

A clear conversation can help you understand what you’re selecting. Bring your questions, even if you’re not sure how to phrase them. You might ask:

  • Which limits are shown in each option?
  • What deductible applies, and when would I pay it?
  • What protections are included or not included?
  • Does the policy information reflect how I use the vehicle?
  • What details should I confirm before the policy is arranged?

Ask for plain explanations if insurance language feels unfamiliar. It’s reasonable to take time to understand what a policy describes. If two options have different costs, ask what differs in the coverage or other details rather than assuming the more affordable one provides the same protections.

What to prepare for a coverage conversation

Have the vehicle’s year, make and model available, along with information about who will drive it and how it’s used. If you already have insurance, a copy of the policy or renewal paperwork can give us a starting point for discussion. If you’re shopping for a vehicle and haven’t chosen one yet, explain what you know so far and when you expect to make a decision.

Be straightforward about changes, such as a different vehicle or a change in regular use. If you’re unsure whether a detail matters, bring it up instead of leaving it out. Accurate information helps keep the discussion focused on options that fit the situation you’ve described.

When it helps to contact an agency

Call when you’re planning a vehicle purchase, reviewing a renewal or trying to understand a policy you already have. You can also contact us if you want to explore options from more than one carrier or need help identifying which questions to ask.
